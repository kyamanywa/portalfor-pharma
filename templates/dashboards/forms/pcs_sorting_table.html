INSPECTION AND SORTING PROCEDURE
1. Ensure that Line Clearance is done before starting the operation as per SOP.
2. Check the Environmental Conditions: Temperature NMT 28°C and Relative Humidity 40-65%.
3. Feed the coated tablets on to the Inspection Belt & sort out the defective tablets.
4. Pass the inspected tablets through Vibro Sifter fitted with appropriate mesh.
5. Pass the inspected tablets through Metal Detector and collect in a clean polybag lined container.
6. Weigh the inspected & sorted tablets and record the weight.
7. Collect the rejected tablets separately and weigh the rejected tablets and record.
8. Affix the label on the containers containing inspected & sorted tablets with Product Name, Batch No, Mfg. Date, Exp. Date, Net Weight and sign.
9. Transfer the inspected and sorted tablets to SMFPQ Store.
