INSPECTION AND SORTING PROCEDURE
  1. Ensure that the inspection area and equipment are clean.
  2. Ensure that all personnel are properly dressed in protective garments, gloves and masks.
  3. Carry out a line clearance exercise as per SOP and complete the "beginning activities" section.
  4. Bring the tablets inside the sorting room.
  5. Visually inspect the tablets for defects such as chipping, spots etc.
  6. Collect all rejected tablets in properly labeled designated containers.
  7. Seal inspected tablets in polythene lined drums and properly label the drums.
  8. Complete the "Ending activities" section of the line clearance form.
  9. Transfer inspected and sorted tablets to Packing area/SMFPQ store.
